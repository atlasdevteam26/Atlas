# Privacy Policy for Atlas

*Last updated: October 13, 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Service and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

We use Your Personal Information to provide and improve the Service. We collect, use and disclose Your information as described in this Privacy Policy, with Your knowledge and consent, except where the law allows otherwise. We handle Personal Information in accordance with Canada's *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA) and other applicable privacy laws.

Atlas is built to work offline. Most of what You create in the Application — Your finds, their locations, photos, voice notes, hikes and profile — is stored only on Your Device and is never sent to Us.

## Collecting and Using Your Personal Information

### Information You Give Us

You can use the map and most features of the Application without an Account. If You create an Account, We collect:

- Email address
- Password

These are sent to Our server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and are used only to create, sign in to and protect Your Account.

### Profile Confirmation

To confirm Your profile, the Application may place a confirmation call inside the Application through Apple Push Notification service. For this, Your Device's push notification token and the Application's identifier are sent to Our server. We use them only to deliver the confirmation call.

### Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following information is created and kept on Your Device. We do not receive it, and it is not sent to Our servers or to Our analytics provider:

- Your profile: name, phone number, short bio and profile picture
- Your finds and their locations, hikes, distance and step counts
- Photos You take of Your finds
- Voice notes and their text transcripts
- Pantry records and the season harvest card
- The forest buddy You choose from Your contacts (their name and phone number)

Deleting this content in the Application, or deleting the Application, removes it from Your Device.

### Device Permissions

Some features ask for access to parts of Your Device. Each permission is requested only when You first use the feature that needs it, and You can turn it on or off at any time in Your Device settings.

- **Location** — to show where You are on the offline map, measure Your hike and save the spots of Your finds. Location is used only while You are using the Application.
- **Camera** — to photograph Your finds and take a profile picture.
- **Photos** — You can choose a profile picture from Your photo library using the system photo picker; the Application receives only the photo You select. The Application can also save Your season harvest card to Photos (add only) and cannot read Your other photos.
- **Microphone and speech recognition** — to record voice notes and turn them into text. Recognition runs on Your Device whenever the Device supports it; otherwise the recording is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
- **Contacts** — to let You pick a forest buddy. Only the contact You choose is saved, on Your Device.
- **Calendar (add only)** — to add a reminder about the start of a season. The Application cannot read Your existing events.
- **Face ID** — to lock Your Finds tab. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS; We never receive Your biometric data.
- **Bluetooth** — to find nearby headphones and speakers so You can play Your voice notes on a hike.
- **Notifications** — to show the profile confirmation call and related alerts.
- **Tracking (App Tracking Transparency)** — see "Analytics" below.

### Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. When You sign in or otherwise contact Our server, Our server records technical information such as Your Device's IP address and the date and time of the request.

### Analytics

We use AppMetrica, a mobile analytics service provided by Yandex ("AppMetrica"), to understand how the Application is used. AppMetrica acts as a Service Provider and processes this data on Our behalf.

**What We send to AppMetrica.** Only pseudonymized usage events: the name of the screen You open, the name of the action You perform on that screen, the Application version and similar non-identifying technical parameters. We do not send Your email address, name or phone number, and We do not send the contents of the finds, notes, photos, voice notes or locations You save in the Application.

**What AppMetrica collects automatically.** As is typical for analytics SDKs, AppMetrica also collects technical data, which may include a resettable device identifier, device model, operating system version, language and region settings, mobile network information, IP address and approximate location derived from it, session start and end times, and crash and error diagnostics. If You allow tracking when the Application asks, AppMetrica may also receive Your Device's advertising identifier. We use this data only in aggregated form to produce usage statistics. We do not use it to identify You personally or to build advertising profiles about You.

**Where the data is processed.** AppMetrica processes data on servers operated by Yandex, which are located outside of Canada — please see "Transfer of Your Personal Information" below.

**Your choices.** You can refuse or withdraw permission to track in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and reset Your advertising identifier at any time. Further information on how AppMetrica processes data is available in its own privacy policy at <https://appmetrica.yandex.com/about/privacy-policy>.

## Use of Your Personal Information

The Organization may use Personal Information for the following purposes:

- **To provide and maintain Our Service**, including to monitor the usage of Our Service.
- **To manage Your Account:** to manage Your registration as a user of the Service and to confirm Your profile.
- **To contact You:** to reply to Your requests and, when necessary, to inform You about changes to the Service or this Privacy Policy, including security updates.
- **To manage Your requests:** to attend to and manage Your requests to Us.
- **To improve the Service:** to analyze usage trends and find and fix errors.

We do not sell Your Personal Information, and We do not use it for advertising.

We may share Your Personal Information in the following situations:

- **With Service Providers:** to host Our server, to deliver push notifications and to analyze the use of Our Service. Our analytics Service Provider is AppMetrica (Yandex), as described in "Analytics" above. Service Providers may use Personal Information only to perform services for Us.
- **For organizational changes:** if the Organization merges with, or transfers the Service to, another organization, Personal Information may be transferred as part of that change. We will notify You before Your Personal Information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
- **With Your consent:** We may disclose Your Personal Information for any other purpose with Your consent.

## Retention of Your Personal Information

The Organization will retain Your Personal Information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, and to the extent necessary to comply with Our legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce Our agreements and policies. Where possible, We delete, aggregate or anonymize data sooner. The periods below are maximum periods:

- **Account information** (email address, password): for as long as Your Account exists. When You delete Your Account, it is removed from Our server; residual copies in backups are deleted on Our routine backup schedule.
- **Push notification token:** until it is replaced by a new token or Your Account is deleted.
- **Correspondence with Us:** up to 24 months from Our last reply.
- **Server logs** (IP addresses, access times): up to 12 months, for security monitoring and troubleshooting.
- **Analytics data:** up to 24 months, in aggregated or pseudonymized form.

We may retain Personal Information longer only where the law requires it or where it is needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Anonymized statistics that cannot be linked back to You may be kept indefinitely.

## Transfer of Your Personal Information

Your information may be processed and stored outside of Canada, including by Our Service Providers (for example, AppMetrica's servers operated by Yandex, and Apple for push notifications and, where on-device processing is not available, speech recognition). While Your information is in another country, it is subject to that country's laws and may be accessible to its courts, law enforcement and national security authorities.

We require Our Service Providers, by contract or other means, to protect Your information to a standard comparable to the one described in this Privacy Policy.

## Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, You have the right to:

- Ask what Personal Information We hold about You and get a copy of it;
- Ask Us to correct Personal Information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
- Withdraw Your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice — some features may then stop working;
- Ask Us to delete Your Personal Information.

To exercise these rights, contact Us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify Your identity before acting on a request.

If You are not satisfied with Our response, You may file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada: <https://www.priv.gc.ca>. If You live outside Canada, You may also have rights under the laws of the place where You live, and We will honour them as those laws require.

## Delete Your Personal Information

You can delete Your Account at any time in the Application, in Profile. This deletes Your Account and the Personal Information associated with it from Our server. Information stored only on Your Device is removed when You delete it in the Application or delete the Application.

You may also contact Us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any Personal Information that You have provided to Us. Please note that We may need to retain certain information when We have a legal obligation to do so.
